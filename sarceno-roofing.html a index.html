Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a roof replacement take? expand_more
Most residential roof replacements are completed within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the size and material. We prioritize speed without compromising structural integrity.
Will you work with my insurance company? expand_more
Yes, we have a dedicated insurance claims department that works directly with adjusters to ensure you receive the maximum coverage for storm or fire damage.
What kind of materials do you use? expand_more
We use only architectural-grade materials from trusted manufacturers like GAF, Owens Corning, and Boral. Every material is rated for California's specific UV and fire resistance requirements.
Do you offer a warranty? expand_more
We offer a Lifetime Workmanship Warranty on all full replacements, in addition to the 25-50 year manufacturer warranties on the materials themselves.
How much does a new roof cost? expand_more
Cost varies significantly based on square footage, material choice, and roof complexity. We provide a detailed line-item estimate after a free on-site inspection so there are zero surprises.
